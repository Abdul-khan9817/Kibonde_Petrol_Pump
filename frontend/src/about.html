About Us

Kibonde Fuels was founded by Kibonde Mzungu, an entrepreneur passionate about energy solutions in Tanzania. Our company is dedicated to supplying high-quality fuel across the nation, ensuring reliable service to both individual and corporate clients.

From our headquarters in Tanzania, we have partnered with well-known companies and multiple fuel stations, providing consistent and efficient fuel supply. Our mission is to become a leading fuel supplier in every region of Tanzania, supporting economic growth and mobility.

At Kibonde Fuels, we value trust, professionalism, and sustainability. We aim to expand our reach while maintaining strong relationships with our customers and partners.

Our goal is simple: powering Tanzania forward, one fuel delivery at a time.
